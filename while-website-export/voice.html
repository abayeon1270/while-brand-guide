The Effortless Host.

Thoughtful, warm, unhurried. Guide with small invitations and familiar language.

PERSONALITY & VOICE

Thoughtful, warm, and unhurried. Everything is planned with intention, while the experience feels easy.

Use concise, suggestive, pairing-led language. Invite guests to notice what changes; leave specialist wine vocabulary behind.

USE

“Try the gelato, then the wine. Notice what changes.”

AVOID

“Follow these steps for the correct tasting experience.”

USE

“Stay for another taste.”

AVOID

“Hurry. Don’t miss the ultimate luxury experience.”

Service formats & consistent vocabulary +

Four ways to enter

Pairing Menu for Two: the signature three-course ritual.
Seasonal Menu: three new seasonal courses.
Single Pair: a shorter introduction.
Zero-Proof Menu: the same structure without alcohol.

Words we use

Write the name as ’while. Use “gelato,” “pairing,” “Bright Start,” “Soft Middle,” and “Long Finish.” Keep “Coursed Ice Cream Bar” as the category descriptor.

Do not invent prices, locations, availability, ingredients or official partnership claims.
