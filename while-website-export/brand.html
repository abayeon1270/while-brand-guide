An after-dinner ritual

’while is a seated Italian gelato and wine experience for adults 35+, especially couples and close friends who want to continue the evening together.

Three small pairings arrive one at a time: a bright opening, a creamier middle, and a lingering finish. Conversation fills the spaces between.

POSITIONING A Coursed Ice Cream BarA gelato-led tasting occasion between the restaurant and the bar.
OUR PURPOSE

To turn dessert
into a social pause.

Take Your Sweet Time.
THE MANIFESTO

The evening deserves
a little more time.

Time to notice a new flavor, linger over a conversation, and enjoy the company across the table. Some of the moments we want to keep happen after the plates have been cleared, when there is still more to say.

’while makes room for those moments. Italian gelato and wine come together in three thoughtful courses: a cool spoonful, a small sip, and a pause to see how the flavors unfold.

We care about the craft behind each pairing and the comfort of feeling welcome. Come with curiosity. We’ll take care of the details.

Take Your Sweet Time.

VISION

An evening ritual.

Make an unhurried dessert experience a familiar way to continue the evening.

MISSION

One course at a time.

Create thoughtful gelato and Italian wine pairings with room for conversation between them.

AUDIENCE

Time together.

Adults 35+, couples and close friends who value considered food, comfort and approachable discovery.

Three values. Everyday decisions.

STRATEGIC PILLARS
01

Time

Leave room for the evening to unfold.

Sequential service. Spacious layouts. Gentle transitions.
02

Craft

Choose every pairing and material with intention.

Precise typography. Tactile materials. Careful finishing.
03

Comfort

Make discovery easy and welcoming.

Familiar language. Clear information. Intimate seating.
