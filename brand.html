01 / The Brand

An after-dinner ritual.

Italian gelato and wine, served in three small pairings. A bright opening, a creamy middle, and a lingering finish—with room for conversation between them.

Our Foundation

Dessert is a reason to stay.

Purpose

To turn dessert into a social pause.

Vision

To make an unhurried dessert experience a familiar way to continue the evening.

Mission

We create thoughtful gelato and Italian wine pairings, served one course at a time, with room for conversation between them.

People & Positioning

For a little more time.

Who It’s For

Adults aged 35 and older, especially couples and close friends who want to continue spending time together after dinner. Curious about wine, they value thoughtful food, comfortable surroundings, and conversation.

A Coursed Ice Cream Bar

A seated, gelato-led experience that brings the structure of a tasting menu to an intimate after-dinner occasion. Small Italian wine pairings arrive sequentially, giving each course its own moment.

Our Manifesto

We believe the evening
deserves a little more time.

Time to notice a new flavor, linger over a conversation, and enjoy the company across the table. Some of the moments we want to keep happen after the plates have been cleared, when there is still more to say.

’while makes room for those moments. Italian gelato and wine come together in three thoughtful courses: a cool spoonful, a small sip, and a pause to see how the flavors unfold.

Come with curiosity. We’ll take care of the details.

Take Your Sweet Time.
