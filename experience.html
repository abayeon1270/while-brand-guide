Make the pause tangible.

Every touchpoint supports a comfortable progression, from the first welcome to something taken home. The ritual unfolds one pairing at a time.

The Guest Journey

A little more time, together.

  1. Arrive

    Understand the offer through the entrance, signage, and welcome.

  2. Settle In

    Feel comfortable through seating, materials, and a clear menu.

  3. Discover

    Experience three pairings sequentially, with room to notice.

  4. Linger

    Let the final taste and conversation continue.

  5. Take It with You

    Carry the ritual home through packaging and selected objects.

Material & Touch

Make the Identity Tangible.

Terracotta, ceramic serving pieces, textiles, and wine glass charms connect the brand to objects guests touch. Each detail should support comfort, recognition, or interaction.

Prototype charm dimensions, material, attachment, edge finish, weight, and cleaning.

Sound

Leave Room for Conversation.

Piero Piccioni instrumental film scores are the supplied reference direction. Curate gentle cinematic jazz, avoid abrupt changes, and keep conversation intelligible.

Scent

Flavors First. Scents After.

The Aesop concept belongs in a separate hand-washing area. Keep the active tasting space free of ambient fragrance.
