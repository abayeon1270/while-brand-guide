SPACE

Intimate by design.

Small tables, comfortable upholstery, warm indirect light, and visible service. Adapt the footprint for venues, expos or a mobile setup while preserving seated discovery.

TOUCH

Craft you can feel.

Terracotta, ceramic serving pieces, textiles and glass charms make the identity tangible. Prototype weight, attachment, edges and cleaning before production.

SOUND

Leave room to talk.

Italian cinematic jazz and instrumental Piero Piccioni references create a gentle background. Avoid abrupt shifts; keep conversation in the foreground.

SCENT

Flavors first.
Scents after.

Keep added fragrance out of the tasting space. Introduce aromatic hand wash afterward, in a separate wash area.

A gentle arrival. An easy transition.
